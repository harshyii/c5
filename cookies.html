Cookie & browser-storage policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

CLOTH X CLUB uses browser storage and, where configured, cookies or similar technologies to keep the store working and remember choices.

Essential storage

Essential browser storage may be used for cart contents, wishlist, checkout continuity, basic preferences and security. These functions may not work if essential storage is blocked or cleared.

Analytics and advertising

This site may enable analytics or advertising technologies in future. If enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure usage or personalize advertising subject to their settings, notices and applicable law. This policy should be updated before non-essential tracking is activated.

Managing cookies

You can block or clear cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. This may remove saved cart or wishlist data and affect site functionality. The consent controls shown on the website are for preference recording and do not substitute for configuring third-party services.
