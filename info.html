Returns, Refunds & Exchanges

Return requests are reviewed under our published policy. Products accepted for discretionary return must generally be unused, unworn, unwashed and unaltered. Exchanges are generally considered for verified manufacturing defects only, subject to applicable law.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Browse the shop, select a product and its available options, add it to your cart and follow the checkout instructions.

When will my order be shipped?

Orders are processed after payment confirmation or verification. Processing and delivery times may vary. Please refer to the shipping policy and any updates provided for your order.

How can I track my order?

Where tracking is supported, shipment details may be shared after dispatch. Use the tracking reference provided to check progress with the relevant courier.

Can I return an item?

Return requests are subject to the applicable return period, product condition, approval and mandatory consumer rights. Please read the Returns Policy before sending an item back.

Can I exchange an item for another size or colour?

Exchanges are generally considered only for verified manufacturing defects, except where applicable law or written approval provides otherwise.

How do I contact customer support?

Use the live chat or chatbot available on our official website.
