A little about us

Everyday essentials.
Uncomplicated streetwear.

CLOTH X CLUB is built around the belief that personal style should feel natural. We focus on wearable essentials, relaxed silhouettes and versatile pieces that can become part of your everyday rotation.

The CLOTH X CLUB point of view

We believe a good wardrobe is not measured by how much it contains, but by how often its pieces become the ones you reach for. CLOTH X CLUB brings together contemporary streetwear and everyday clothing with an emphasis on ease, considered proportions and uncomplicated styling.

Our collection is designed to be mixed, repeated and made personal. Whether you are looking for a relaxed T-shirt, a dependable everyday layer or an accessory that completes the look, our aim is to make shopping clear and straightforward: accurate product information, visible pricing, accessible customer support and policies that explain what to expect.

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, open the product you want, review the description and available options, add the item to your bag and follow the checkout instructions. The final price and applicable delivery charges should be reviewed before confirming an order.

How do I know whether a product is available?

Availability is displayed on the product page where current stock information is provided. Availability may change while an item is in your bag; an item is not reserved until the order is accepted under the applicable checkout process.

How much is delivery?

Free delivery is offered on eligible orders above ₹1,499 within the applicable service area. Any delivery fee or other charge applicable to your order should be shown before you submit the order.

Can I exchange an item?

Exchanges are considered for verified manufacturing defects only, subject to inspection and applicable consumer rights. A change of mind, size preference or colour preference does not by itself create an exchange entitlement unless a specific offer or applicable law provides otherwise.

How do I report a manufacturing defect?

Contact customer care promptly with your order reference, a description of the issue and clear photographs or video showing the reported defect. Please do not wash, wear, alter or repair the item before the review is completed.

Where can I read the policies?

See our Shipping Policy, Returns & Refunds Policy, Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
