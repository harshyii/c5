FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How can I place an order?

Browse our collection, select your preferred product, choose a size or colour if available, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout.

How can I know which size to choose?

Check the size information provided on the product page before placing your order. If you need additional help choosing a size, contact our support team.

How much do I need to spend for free delivery?

Orders of ₹1,499 or above qualify for free delivery. Your cart will help you track how much more you need to add to unlock free shipping.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

If you need to change or cancel your order, please contact us as soon as possible. Requests are subject to the current order status and our applicable policies.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, tracking information will be shared when available.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Cash on Delivery may be available for eligible orders. Check the payment options displayed during checkout.

What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

Contact our support team with your order details and clear images of the issue so we can assist you.

How can I contact CLOTH X CLUB?

You can reach us through the contact details listed in the Contact section of this page.
