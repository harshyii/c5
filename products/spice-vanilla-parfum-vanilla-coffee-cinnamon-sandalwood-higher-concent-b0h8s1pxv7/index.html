Experience fragrance beyond ordinary EDP perfumes with Maison De Profumo Spice Vanilla PARFUM — a luxurious gourmand fragrance crafted with 22-23% perfume oil concentration for a richer, deeper and more refined scent experience. Unlike regular EDP fragrances, Spice Vanilla is designed as a PARFUM, allowing the fragrance to evolve beautifully on skin with enhanced depth, warmth and lasting elegance. The higher perfume concentration creates a sophisticated scent trail that feels intimate, luxurious and memorable throughout the day. Spice Vanilla is crafted for modern fragrance lovers who appreciate premium perfumery, refined ingredients and original compositions over ordinary mass fragrances. Its warm gourmand character makes it ideal for evening wear, romantic occasions, luxury daily wear, winter evenings and signature scent lovers.
