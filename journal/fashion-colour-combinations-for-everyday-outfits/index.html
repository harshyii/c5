Easy Colour Combinations for Everyday Outfits

Explore simple black, white, grey, olive and denim combinations for easy everyday outfits.

A simple colour palette can make getting dressed easier. Neutral shades are easy to combine and provide a useful base for everyday streetwear outfits.

Black and white create a crisp contrast. Try a black oversized tee with light trousers, or a white tee with black cargos. Keep footwear neutral to maintain a clean finish.

Grey works well with black, white and denim. Olive can add colour without making an outfit feel too loud, especially when paired with cream, charcoal or washed black.

Use these combinations as starting points rather than strict rules. Experiment with the pieces you already own and choose colours that feel right to you.
