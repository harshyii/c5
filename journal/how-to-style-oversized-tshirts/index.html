How to Style Oversized T-Shirts

Learn how to style oversized T-shirts with balanced proportions, simple layers and everyday streetwear essentials.

An oversized T-shirt is one of the easiest foundations for an everyday streetwear outfit. Its relaxed shape gives you room to experiment while keeping the look comfortable.

Start with proportion. Pair a roomy tee with straight-fit or relaxed trousers. If both pieces are very wide, keep the colours simple so the outfit feels intentional rather than bulky.

For an easy layered look, add an open shirt, lightweight jacket or overshirt. Finish with clean sneakers and one or two understated accessories. Let the fit do most of the work.

The best outfit is one you feel comfortable wearing. Try different combinations, keep the pieces that work for you, and make the look your own.
