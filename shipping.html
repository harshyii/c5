Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information about order processing, shipping, delivery and fulfilment.

Last Updated: 21 November 2026

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how CLOTH X CLUB processes and delivers orders placed through our website. By placing an order, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this policy, subject to your rights under applicable law.

1. Business and Shipping Origin

CLOTH X CLUB operates from the following business address:

Cloth x Club
CLOTH x CLUB
Pawan Market, Near Shitla Mata Mandir
Pehowa, Haryana 136128
India

Orders are processed through our fulfilment arrangements. The actual dispatch location may vary depending on inventory, logistics arrangements and the destination of the order.

2. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed after successful payment confirmation or verification of the selected payment method.

Processing time may vary depending on product availability, order volume, verification requirements, holidays and other operational circumstances.

Placing an order does not mean that it has already been dispatched. Orders may require additional processing before being handed over to a shipping or courier partner.

If an order cannot be fulfilled due to stock unavailability, an operational issue or another valid reason, we may contact the customer to discuss the available options, including cancellation and an applicable refund.

3. Shipping Charges and Free Delivery

CLOTH X CLUB offers free delivery on eligible orders with a merchandise value above ₹1,499, subject to the shipping terms displayed at checkout.

Orders below the applicable free-delivery threshold may attract shipping or handling charges. Any applicable charges will be displayed during checkout before the customer completes the purchase.

Shipping charges, where applicable, may vary based on the delivery destination, shipment characteristics, courier service and other relevant factors.

Any promotional shipping benefit is subject to its stated eligibility conditions and validity period.

4. Delivery Estimates

Delivery estimates, where available, are indicative and may vary according to the delivery address, courier availability, transport conditions, weather, public holidays, local restrictions and other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

A delivery estimate is not a guaranteed delivery date unless expressly confirmed as such by CLOTH X CLUB in writing.

Customers should ensure that the delivery address, postal code, contact number and other required details entered at checkout are accurate and complete.

5. Shipment Tracking

Where tracking is supported by the assigned courier or logistics provider, tracking details may be shared with the customer after dispatch.

Tracking updates are provided by the relevant logistics provider and may not appear immediately after shipment registration. Customers may use the supplied tracking reference to follow the shipment's progress.

6. Delivery Attempts and Incorrect Address Details

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery information and ensuring that someone is available to receive the shipment where required.

If delivery is unsuccessful because of an incorrect address, incomplete contact information, recipient unavailability, refusal to accept delivery or other recipient-related reasons, the courier may make another delivery attempt or return the shipment to its origin, depending on its procedures.

Additional shipping costs or other consequences arising from incorrect information supplied by the customer may be the customer's responsibility, where permitted by applicable law and communicated to the customer.

7. Delayed, Lost or Damaged Shipments

Although we work with logistics partners to facilitate delivery, delays may occur due to weather conditions, transport disruptions, operational issues, public holidays, regulatory checks or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

If a shipment appears delayed, lost or damaged during transit, customers should contact our support team with their order number and relevant details.

We may coordinate with the courier or logistics provider to investigate the matter and determine the appropriate next steps. Any remedy will be handled in accordance with the circumstances and applicable consumer protection requirements.

8. International Shipping

International delivery availability depends on the destination, product eligibility, logistics arrangements and applicable import and export requirements.

An international order is accepted only where the destination is supported and the available shipping options are displayed or confirmed during the ordering process.

International shipments may be subject to customs inspections, import duties, taxes, clearance charges or other fees imposed by the destination country.

Unless expressly stated otherwise at checkout or required by applicable law, such destination-country charges are the responsibility of the recipient.

Customs clearance and local delivery timelines are controlled by the relevant authorities and logistics providers and are outside the direct control of CLOTH X CLUB.

9. Address Changes and Order Cancellation

Customers who need to correct or update a delivery address should contact our support team as soon as possible after placing the order.

Address changes and cancellation requests can be considered only before the order reaches a stage at which the change is no longer operationally possible.

Once an order has been dispatched or handed over to a courier, we may be unable to cancel the shipment or change its destination.

10. Delivery Confirmation

Courier tracking records, proof of delivery, delivery scans or other records maintained by the logistics provider may be used to investigate delivery-related enquiries.

If a customer believes an order has been incorrectly marked as delivered or has not been received, they should notify our support team promptly so that the matter can be investigated.

11. Customer Support

For shipping, delivery, tracking or order-related assistance, customers may contact CLOTH X CLUB through the official website:

Website: https://harshyii.github.io/clothxclub/

Live chat and chatbot support are available through our website, subject to service availability.

Preferred support method: Live chat.

12. Policy Updates and Applicable Law

CLOTH X CLUB may update this Shipping & Delivery Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.

The policy applicable to an order will be interpreted together with the order details, checkout disclosures and applicable law. Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

In the event of any inconsistency between this policy and mandatory legal requirements, the applicable legal requirements will prevail.
