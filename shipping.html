Delivery, clearly explained

Shipping & delivery.

From order confirmation to your doorstep, here is how we handle dispatch, delivery charges, tracking and delivery-related questions.

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Applies to orders placed through the CLOTH X CLUB website.

1. Business and shipping origin

CLOTH X CLUB is a proprietorship firm operating from Pawan Market, Near Shitla Mata Mandir, Pehowa, Haryana 136128, India. Orders are processed from our operating location or an authorised fulfilment location used for the relevant order. The dispatch origin may vary where fulfilment arrangements require it.

2. Order processing

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation or confirmation of an offered payment-on-delivery arrangement, where available. Processing time is separate from transit time. We make reasonable efforts to prepare accepted orders promptly, but dispatch may take longer during high-volume periods, public holidays, stock verification, adverse weather, courier disruptions or other operational circumstances.

An order confirmation is an acknowledgement of the order details and is not necessarily proof that the parcel has been handed to the courier. Dispatch or tracking information will be shared when it becomes available through the order communication channel.

3. Delivery charges and free-delivery threshold

Free standard delivery is offered on eligible domestic orders with a merchandise value above ₹1,499, unless a product page, campaign or checkout disclosure states a different condition. Orders below the threshold may attract a shipping charge. The applicable delivery fee, if any, must be displayed in the cart or checkout before the customer confirms the purchase.

Promotional shipping offers may be subject to exclusions, serviceability, minimum order values or other conditions displayed at the time of the offer. We will not treat an undisclosed charge as accepted by the customer.

4. Delivery estimates and serviceability

Delivery estimates shown on the website or communicated after ordering are indicative unless expressly stated as guaranteed. Actual transit time depends on destination PIN code, courier capacity, weather, local restrictions and other events outside our reasonable control. Serviceability is checked against the delivery address and courier coverage. If a location cannot be served after an order is placed, we will contact the customer to arrange an appropriate resolution, including cancellation and refund where applicable.

5. Customer delivery information

Please provide a complete and accurate recipient name, mobile number, PIN code, locality, city, state and address details. Customers should ensure that the recipient or an authorised person is available to receive the parcel. Incorrect or incomplete address details, unreachable contact numbers or repeated failed delivery attempts may cause delays or return-to-origin handling.

6. Tracking and delivery updates

Where courier tracking is supported, the tracking reference or link may be shared after dispatch. Courier scans may not appear immediately and can take time to update. If tracking has not changed for an unusually long period or the parcel appears delivered but has not been received, contact customer care with your order number.

7. Delays, failed delivery and returned parcels

Delivery may be delayed by events such as extreme weather, transport disruption, strikes, public restrictions, security checks, incorrect address details or courier operational issues. We will make reasonable efforts to assist with shipment enquiries. Where a parcel is returned because delivery could not be completed, we may contact the customer to verify the address and discuss re-dispatch or cancellation, subject to the circumstances and applicable law.

8. Damaged, tampered or incorrect parcels

If the outer package appears visibly damaged or tampered with at delivery, please document the condition with photographs or video where practical and notify customer care as soon as possible. If you receive an incorrect item or a product with a reported manufacturing defect, contact us with the order reference and supporting details. This reporting request helps us investigate and does not remove any statutory rights.

9. International delivery

International delivery, including to the United States or Canada, is available only where the destination is enabled at checkout or expressly confirmed by CLOTH X CLUB. International shipping charges, delivery estimates, customs duties, import taxes and other destination-country charges will be disclosed where known and applicable. Unless expressly stated otherwise at checkout, customs duties and import charges imposed by the destination country are the customer's responsibility. International delivery may be subject to local import rules and courier restrictions.

10. Contact

For shipping assistance, contact clothxclub@gmail.com or call / WhatsApp +91 99915 03210. Business address: Pawan Market, Near Shitla Mata Mandir, Pehowa, Haryana 136128, India.

These terms are intended to explain our delivery process and do not limit remedies available under applicable consumer-protection law.
