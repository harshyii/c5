Returns, Refunds & Exchanges

Our policy for returns, exceptional requests and manufacturing defects.

Last Updated: 21 November 2026

At CLOTH X CLUB, we aim to provide products that meet our quality standards and customer expectations. This Returns, Refunds & Exchanges Policy explains the circumstances in which a return, refund or exchange request may be considered.

By placing an order through our website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. This policy is subject to applicable consumer protection laws and does not limit any rights that cannot legally be excluded.

1. General Return Policy

Returns are not automatically available for every product or order. A return request may be considered at the sole discretion of CLOTH X CLUB, subject to the product category, order details, geographical location, membership status and circumstances of the request.

Unless a different return period is expressly confirmed in writing by CLOTH X CLUB, customers should contact our support team promptly after receiving their order if they wish to request a return. Approval must be obtained before sending any item back.

2. Exceptional Return Period – Up to 365 Days

In certain exceptional circumstances, CLOTH X CLUB may approve an extended return period of up to 365 days from the date of delivery.

This extended period is not a standard or automatic return entitlement. It may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • Eligible customers holding an active CLOTH X CLUB membership or qualifying Club Membership benefits.
  • Specific customer arrangements or written approvals issued by CLOTH X CLUB.
  • Eligible orders placed in Canada or the United States, where an extended return arrangement has been expressly offered or confirmed for the relevant order.
  • Other exceptional situations accepted by CLOTH X CLUB on a case-by-case basis.

The availability, duration and conditions of an extended return period may vary depending on the membership terms, country, product, order date and individual circumstances. An extended period applies only when confirmed by CLOTH X CLUB and does not automatically apply to all customers or purchases in Canada, the United States or any other country.

3. Product Condition Requirements

Any product accepted for return must satisfy the following requirements, unless applicable law requires otherwise:

  • The product must be unused, unworn, unwashed and unaltered.
  • The product must be free from stains, odours, damage, marks or signs of use.
  • All original tags, labels, accessories and packaging should be included, where supplied.
  • The product must be returned in a condition suitable for inspection and resale.
  • Proof of purchase or a valid order reference must be provided.

CLOTH X CLUB reserves the right to inspect returned products and decline a discretionary return if the item does not satisfy the applicable condition requirements.

4. Exchanges – Manufacturing Defects Only

Exchanges are generally considered only where a product is found to have a genuine manufacturing defect.

We do not ordinarily offer exchanges for reasons such as a change of mind, personal preference, incorrect size selection, colour preference or ordering the wrong product, except where required by applicable law or expressly approved by CLOTH X CLUB.

A manufacturing defect claim may be subject to inspection and verification. Customers may be asked to provide clear photographs, videos, packaging details and the relevant order number.

If a manufacturing defect is verified, CLOTH X CLUB may, depending on product availability and applicable law, offer an eligible replacement or another appropriate remedy.

Damage resulting from misuse, accidental damage, improper washing, incorrect care, normal wear and tear, unauthorised alterations or handling after delivery will not ordinarily qualify as a manufacturing defect.

5. Refunds

A refund is not guaranteed merely because a return request has been submitted. Refunds may be issued only after the return has been approved and the returned product, where applicable, has been received and inspected.

Where a refund is approved, it will ordinarily be processed through the original payment method or another method agreed upon with the customer, subject to payment provider limitations and applicable law.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution.

Any shipping charges, duties, taxes or other fees will be handled in accordance with the applicable order terms and legal requirements. Where a refund is legally required, it will not be withheld contrary to applicable law.

6. Shipping and Return Costs

Unless otherwise confirmed in writing or required by applicable law, customers may be responsible for the cost of shipping an approved discretionary return to CLOTH X CLUB.

For verified manufacturing defects, incorrect items supplied or other circumstances where the law requires the seller to bear the cost, CLOTH X CLUB will make appropriate arrangements in accordance with applicable requirements.

Customers should not dispatch a return without receiving return instructions from our support team. Unauthorised returns may be delayed or may not be identifiable upon receipt.

7. Items That May Not Qualify for Return

Subject to applicable consumer rights, the following items or circumstances may be excluded from discretionary returns:

  • Products that have been worn, washed, used or altered.
  • Products damaged after delivery due to customer handling or misuse.
  • Products without required tags, accessories or original components, where their absence affects eligibility.
  • Products specifically identified as non-returnable before purchase, where legally permitted.
  • Requests that fall outside the applicable return period and have not received an exceptional written approval.

8. How to Request a Return or Report a Defect

To request a return or report a suspected manufacturing defect, contact our customer support team using the contact details published on our website.

Please provide the following information:

  • Your full name and registered email address.
  • Your order number and date of purchase.
  • The product name and relevant size or variant.
  • The reason for your request.
  • Clear photographs or videos, where relevant.

Our team will review the request and communicate the next steps. Please wait for written return authorisation and instructions before shipping any product.

9. Policy Changes and Case-by-Case Decisions

CLOTH X CLUB reserves the right to amend, update or revise this policy from time to time. The policy applicable to an order will be determined in accordance with applicable law and the terms communicated to the customer at the time of purchase.

Return requests, exceptional extensions, membership benefits and other special circumstances may be assessed individually. Any discretionary approval must be communicated by an authorised CLOTH X CLUB representative.

Nothing in this policy gives CLOTH X CLUB the right to deny a customer's mandatory rights under applicable consumer protection legislation.

10. Contact Us

For questions about returns, refunds, membership-related exceptions or manufacturing defects, please contact us through the official contact details listed on our Contact page.
