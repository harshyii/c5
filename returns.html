A fair and transparent process

Returns, refunds
& exchanges.

We want you to understand exactly when a return may be considered, what condition an item must be in and how to raise a product concern.

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Please read before placing an order.

1. Scope and important distinction

This policy applies to purchases made directly through the CLOTH X CLUB website. A return, refund and exchange are different outcomes. A return is the process of sending an item back; a refund is repayment of an eligible amount; an exchange is replacement of an item. Approval of one outcome does not automatically mean approval of another.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law. Where mandatory law provides a stronger right, that law will prevail over any inconsistent wording in this policy.

2. Standard condition of returned items

Unless the item is being returned because of a verified defect, damage or other reason for which applicable law requires a different treatment, any item accepted for a discretionary return must be unused, unworn, unwashed, unaltered and free from stains, odours, damage, pet hair or signs of use. Original tags, labels, accessories, packaging and proof of purchase should be included where supplied.

For hygiene, product integrity and resale reasons, an item that has been worn, washed, altered, repaired, damaged after delivery or returned without essential components may not qualify for a discretionary return. This condition does not prevent a customer from raising a genuine manufacturing-defect or statutory claim.

3. Standard returns and special approval

CLOTH X CLUB does not offer an unconditional, universal 365-day change-of-mind return promise on every order. A return outside the standard eligibility displayed for a particular product or order may be considered only in special circumstances, at our discretion, or where required by applicable law.

Special circumstances may include an active CLOTH X CLUB Club Membership benefit that expressly provides an extended return window, a specific written approval issued by customer care, or an international order to the United States or Canada for which an extended return arrangement is expressly offered and shown at the time of purchase.

4. Extended return period — up to 365 days in qualifying cases

An extended return period of up to 365 days from the date of delivery may be available only where the customer and order meet the conditions of a specific Club Membership benefit, written exception or eligible United States / Canada programme confirmed by CLOTH X CLUB. The applicable duration may be shorter than 365 days and will be the duration expressly communicated for that membership, order or programme.

To rely on an extended return benefit, the customer may be asked to provide the order number, membership identification or the written confirmation that granted the benefit. The benefit is personal to the eligible order or member, cannot be assumed from the mere existence of a membership, and may be subject to exclusions disclosed when the benefit is offered.

For orders delivered in the United States or Canada, an extended return window is not automatic solely because of the destination country. It applies only if the order or checkout page expressly identifies the applicable international return programme and its conditions. Local mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

5. Exchanges — manufacturing defects only

As a store policy, exchanges are considered only for a verified manufacturing defect, such as a material or stitching fault that existed at the time the product was supplied. Exchanges are not ordinarily provided for a change of mind, incorrect size selection, colour preference, styling preference or an item ordered by mistake, except where a separate written offer or applicable law provides otherwise.

Where a manufacturing defect is verified, we may offer an exchange or another remedy required or permitted by applicable law, subject to product availability and the customer's legal rights. If a replacement is unavailable, we will discuss an appropriate resolution, which may include a refund where required or agreed.

6. How to report a defect or request a return

Contact us at clothxclub@gmail.com or WhatsApp / call +91 99915 03210. Include:

  • Your order number and date of delivery.
  • The product name and size / colour, if relevant.
  • A clear description of the reason for the request.
  • Clear photographs or video showing the issue, where the request concerns damage or a manufacturing defect.
  • Your Club Membership or written extended-return approval details, if you are relying on a special benefit.

Please contact us before dispatching an item back. Unannounced parcels may be difficult to identify and process. We will provide return instructions and the applicable return address after reviewing the request.

7. Inspection and decision

Returned items may be inspected to verify the order, product identity, condition and reported issue. A decision will be based on the evidence, product condition, applicable programme terms and consumer law. We will communicate the outcome and, where a request is not approved, the reason for that decision. A request for photographs or inspection is an administrative step and does not remove statutory rights.

8. Return shipping and costs

Where a return or exchange is approved because of a verified manufacturing defect, incorrect item supplied or another issue attributable to us, we will provide instructions on the reasonable return method and applicable shipping arrangements. For discretionary returns approved as a special exception, return shipping costs and any other permitted costs will be communicated before the customer proceeds. No charge will be imposed without appropriate disclosure and consent, and mandatory legal obligations will prevail.

9. Refund method and timing

Where a refund is approved or legally required, it will ordinarily be processed to the original payment method where feasible, or by another lawful method agreed with the customer. The time for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank or card issuer. We will initiate the refund within the period required by applicable law or the relevant payment-provider rules and communicate any available reference.

Shipping charges, cash-on-delivery fees, duties or other third-party charges may be treated according to the disclosed checkout terms and applicable law. We will not withhold an amount where doing so would violate a mandatory consumer right.

10. Orders delivered outside India

For eligible US and Canada orders, the return destination, international postage, customs documentation, duties and refund currency or conversion treatment will be communicated when the return is approved. Unless applicable law or a written programme says otherwise, international return shipping and customs costs for discretionary returns may be the customer's responsibility. Any special international programme will state its own return window and exclusions.

11. Items not eligible for discretionary return

Subject to statutory rights, discretionary returns may be declined for items that are used, washed, worn, altered, damaged after delivery, missing essential tags or components, or not linked to a verifiable order. Items specifically identified as final sale or non-returnable may also be excluded from discretionary returns where lawful and clearly disclosed before purchase. These exclusions do not apply to the extent they would unlawfully restrict a remedy for defective, misdescribed or otherwise non-conforming goods.

12. Policy changes and individual circumstances

We may revise this policy prospectively. The policy and programme terms disclosed at the time of purchase will generally apply to that order, subject to applicable law. CLOTH X CLUB may consider individual circumstances and provide a written exception; an exception for one order does not create an automatic entitlement for another order or customer.
