Membership Q&A

Questions? Here's a place to start.

How do I apply to join?

Complete the joining form above. If it doesn't load, open it directly using the Google Forms link.

Is submitting the form an automatic confirmation?

Submitting the form is your application/registration submission. Any confirmation or membership details will be provided through the club's follow-up process.

What information should I submit?

Provide only the information requested in the form and make sure your contact details are accurate so the club can reach you if needed.

Who can I contact if I have questions?

Email clothxclub@gmail.com or contact us through the Contact page.

Can I edit my response after submitting?

Whether responses can be edited depends on the Google Form's settings. Use any edit-response link Google Forms provides after submission, if available.
