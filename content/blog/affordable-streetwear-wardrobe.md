A streetwear wardrobe does not need to be large. Focus on versatile pieces, thoughtful purchases and combinations you can wear repeatedly.

## Prioritise the basics
Start with T-shirts and bottoms that work across multiple outfits. Choose pieces based on what you regularly wear.

## Select versatile colours
Neutral colours make outfit combinations easier. Add statement pieces when they fill a genuine gap in your wardrobe.

## Consider cost per wear
A useful item that you wear regularly may offer more value than a piece purchased only for one trend.

## Make combinations before buying
Think about how a new garment works with existing clothing. This can help avoid purchases that are difficult to style.

## Take care of your clothes
Follow garment care instructions to help clothing remain wearable.

## Frequently asked questions
### How can I build streetwear on a budget?
Start with versatile basics, plan combinations and add items gradually.

### What should I buy first?
Choose the pieces you need most often, such as everyday T-shirts and bottoms.

### Is affordable streetwear possible?
Yes. Focus on your needs, fit, versatility and care rather than a particular price label.
