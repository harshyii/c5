A white T-shirt is a useful starting point for casual outfits because it can be paired with many colours and silhouettes.

## White tee with blue jeans
This simple combination works for everyday wear. Choose a denim fit that complements the T-shirt.

## White tee with darker bottoms
Black, charcoal and navy bottoms create contrast. Cargos can make the outfit more utility-inspired.

## Add a layer
An overshirt or jacket can introduce colour and texture. Wear it open for a relaxed look.

## Explore tonal outfits
White, cream and beige can create a soft neutral palette. Different textures help the pieces remain distinct.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do you style a plain white T-shirt?
Try denim, cargos, shorts or casual trousers with footwear that fits the outfit.

### What colour jeans go with a white tee?
Blue, black and grey jeans are easy options.

### How can I make a white tee outfit interesting?
Use fit, layers, accessories, footwear and texture to add variety.
