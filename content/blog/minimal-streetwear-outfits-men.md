Minimal streetwear focuses on simple combinations, useful pieces and thoughtful proportions. It can be expressive without relying on many graphics or colours.

## Build a neutral palette
Black, white, grey, beige and navy are useful. Choose colours that work together across your wardrobe.

## Choose clean silhouettes
Oversized, relaxed or regular-fit tees can all fit a minimal look. Focus on fit and the overall outfit.

## Use texture instead of many colours
Different fabrics and garment shapes can create interest while keeping the palette restrained.

## Keep accessories understated
Simple footwear and accessories can complement the outfit without overwhelming it.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do you create a minimalist streetwear outfit?
Start with neutral colours, simple silhouettes and versatile pieces.

### Is minimal streetwear boring?
Not necessarily. Fit, texture, proportions and small details can make simple outfits distinctive.

### Which colours are best for minimal streetwear?
Neutral colours are a common starting point, but the palette can reflect personal preference.
