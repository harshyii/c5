Privacy Policy

How CLOTH X CLUB handles information when you visit or shop with us.

Last Updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CLOTH X CLUB ("we", "us" or "our") may collect, use, disclose and protect information relating to visitors and customers of our website. It applies to information collected through our website and related customer-support interactions.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information you provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, billing and delivery address, order details, customer-support messages and information you submit when contacting us.

We may also receive technical and usage information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages and interaction data. Cookies or similar technologies may be used where enabled by the website or its service providers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To receive, process, fulfil and provide updates about orders.
  • To provide customer service, live chat and chatbot support.
  • To respond to questions, complaints, return requests and defect reports.
  • To maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To understand website performance and improve products and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.
  • To send marketing communications where permitted and, where required, with your consent.

3. Payments

Payments may be handled by third-party payment processors. We do not intend to store complete payment-card details on this website. Payment providers process payment information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider's information when completing a transaction.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies or browser storage may be used to support essential website functions, remember preferences, maintain shopping-cart information and understand site usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect website functionality.

5. Sharing Information

We may share relevant information with service providers that help operate the website, process payments, deliver orders, provide customer support, host website content, or perform analytics. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business reorganisation. We do not state that every service provider is used at all times; the providers involved depend on the services enabled for your order or visit.

6. International Processing

Depending on the services used and your location, information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we take steps required by relevant data-protection laws for such transfers.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order administration, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also opt out of marketing messages using the unsubscribe mechanism where provided. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to solicit personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so that we can review the request.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Changes apply as permitted by applicable law.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us through the live chat or chatbot available on our website. Please include enough information for us to identify and respond to your request.
