Your information matters

Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of the information we handle, why we handle it and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to CLOTH X CLUB, a proprietorship firm operating from Pawan Market, Near Shitla Mata Mandir, Pehowa, Haryana 136128, India ("CLOTH X CLUB", "we", "us" or "our"). Our website is https://harshyii.github.io/c5/. Privacy enquiries may be sent to clothxclub@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing address, order details, product preferences, membership information, customer-service messages, complaint details and information you submit through forms or live support.

When you browse the website, technical information may be generated or collected by the hosting platform or enabled services, including IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages visited, referring page, approximate location derived from network information, and date or time of access. Cookies or local browser storage may be used for essential functions such as cart state, preferences and security. The exact data collected depends on the hosting, analytics, advertising and payment services actually enabled.

3. How we use information

  • To display products and operate the website.
  • To process orders, communicate delivery updates and provide after-sales support.
  • To manage returns, defect reports, refunds, exchanges and grievances.
  • To respond to enquiries and maintain customer-service records.
  • To maintain website security, detect fraud or misuse and troubleshoot errors.
  • To understand performance and improve our products, content and customer experience.
  • To send promotional messages where permitted by law and where any required consent has been obtained.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, consumer-protection and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal basis and consent

We handle personal information for purposes such as fulfilling a purchase or requested service, responding to your request, complying with legal obligations, protecting legitimate business and customer interests, and obtaining consent where consent is required. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it subject to legal and operational limitations. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

5. Payments and financial information

Payments may be handled by third-party payment service providers. We do not ask customers to send complete card numbers, CVV codes, banking passwords or one-time passwords by email or chat. Payment providers may collect and process payment details under their own privacy notices and security practices. We receive only the transaction information reasonably needed to confirm and administer an order.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Cookies or similar technologies may be used to keep essential website features working, remember preferences, support shopping-cart functionality, understand site performance or support advertising where configured. You can manage cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect cart or site functionality. If analytics or advertising tags are activated, the relevant provider may process usage information under its own terms and settings.

7. When information may be shared

We may share necessary information with website hosting providers, payment processors, courier and fulfilment partners, customer-support providers, analytics or advertising providers where enabled, professional advisers and public authorities where disclosure is required by law. We limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product.

8. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside India. Where information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable law and the contractual arrangements governing the relevant service. International transfers may also be subject to the privacy laws of the country where the service provider operates.

9. Retention

We retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including order administration, customer support, accounting, tax, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legal compliance. Some records may need to be retained for statutory periods even after an account or customer relationship ends.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the services used. No website, internet transmission, cloud service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should use secure devices, protect their email and phone accounts, and never share passwords or one-time passwords with anyone claiming to represent us.

11. Your privacy choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or erasure of personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a concern about how information is handled. We may need to verify your identity and the order or account involved before acting. Some information may be retained where required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Send privacy requests to clothxclub@gmail.com. We will review and respond in accordance with applicable law.

12. Children's information

Our online store is intended for people able to make purchases under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other safeguards are required. If you believe a child has submitted personal information improperly, contact us so that we can review the matter.

13. Third-party links

Our website may link to payment services, courier tracking pages, social media or other third-party websites. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy policies. Review those policies before submitting information to them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements or data practices. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new revision date. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
